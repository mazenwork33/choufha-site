This policy explains how **Choufha**, a brand of REHLA Growth for Business Development & Marketing L.L.C (C.R. 26923), handles data received through this website.

## Data we collect
- **Contact details** you send us voluntarily by email or through the contact form, such as your name, showroom name, phone number and city.
- **General technical data** such as browser and device type, used to improve site performance.

## The AR experience
The augmented reality experience runs entirely on the user's device. We do not record, store or transmit camera images.

## How we use data
Contact details are used only to answer your requests and prepare demos and quotations. We do not sell them or share them with third parties for marketing.

## Your rights
You may ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to [support@choufha.com](mailto:support@choufha.com).

## Changes
We may update this policy from time to time; the last-updated date above applies.
